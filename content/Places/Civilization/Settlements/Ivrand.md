Ivrand is a [[Miria|Mirian]] city in the northeastern corner of [[Astrana]], and is known as the edge of civilization in the northeast, despite the existence of multiple major settlements further both north and east. It is something of an infamous city, with a reputation for being the place one moves to in order to be forgotten about, or to acquire work of a questionable nature, in ethics and source.
### Notable People and Groups
The [[Naya]] purportedly use Ivrand as a kind of recruiting ground, but there is little evidence of this beyond firsthand accounts.
### Geography
Ivrand is situated just south of [[Plaitos Forest]], along the eastern side of the [[Ireborough River]]. There are logging camps situated on the northern side of the city walls, and acres of farms stretch out south along both sides of the Ireborough. 
### History
Ivrand was founded centuries ago by Mirian monks who founded a monastery and established a logging camp, and has grown greatly in the time since, with monks offering a life in devoted service to Miria and the works of her kingdom as a way forward from past mistakes. The original monastery still stands just north of the city wall.

### Population
Recent estimates put its population around 11,000 people. Ivrand is home to a variety of sapient species, including [[Human|humans]], [[Elf|elves]], [[Dwarf|dwarves]], [[Goblin|goblins]], [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]], [[Minotaur|minotaurai]], [[Ratfolk|ratfolk]], and even some [[Goloma|goloma]] and [[Hungerseed|hungerseeds]].
