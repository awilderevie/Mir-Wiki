## Rebirth
One of the most significant events in [[Mir]]’s history is when [[Miria]] herself brought water and life to the [[Great Desert]] at the center of [[Astrana]], widely known as the [[Rebirth]]. This transformation at the very heart of Astrana brought a new unity to the disparate [[Prehistoric Kingdoms|prehistoric kingdoms]] just over a thousand years ago. It was the rebirth of the [[People of Mir|people of Mir]] as whole. 
## The Great Crusade
With her growing influence and ambition, Miria then established the first [[Divine Imperial Army]]. She sent them across the lands in the [[Great Crusade]] to abolish the cruelest practices of the people of the world, like slavery and child labor. Miria hoped to liberate all sentient life from the oppression designed by those in power to stay in power indefinitely. This was a two hundred year long crusade across every corner of Astrana.

Historians say Miria used the resulting power vacuum to further establish her influence in all of the kingdoms, sending a deluge of loyal devotees to help fix the resulting economic and agricultural collapse. These devotees built more humane practices in the old ones’ stead, while naturally also building in heavy taxation for the divine empire. Her loyalists represented her interests in the kingdoms’ armies, navies, and royal guards. 
## The Age of the Empire
Miria's unification of the people of the world had its limits, though; naturally, new wars began, alliances and hostilities took shape, and conflict broke out anew throughout the realms of the world. In the aftermath of the Great Crusade, Miria reformed the Divine Imperial Army as the [[Watch]], a reformed imperial force that watched over the peoples throughout Astrana as so-called peacekeepers. The era of history where most populous realms existed under Miria's Watch is known as the [[Age of the Empire]].
