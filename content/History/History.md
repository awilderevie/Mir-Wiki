## Rebirth
One of the most significant events in [[Mir]]’s history is when [[Miria]] herself brought water and life to the Great Desert at the center of [[Astrana]], widely known as the [[Rebirth]]. This transformation at the very heart of Astrana brought a heretofore unseen unity to the disparate [[Prehistoric Kingdoms|prehistoric kingdoms]] some four and a half thousand years ago. It was the rebirth of the [[People of Mir|people of Mir]] as whole. 
## The Great Crusade
With her growing influence and ambition, Miria then established the first [[Divine Imperial Army]]. She sent them across the lands in the [[Great Crusade]] to abolish the cruelest practices of the people of the world, like slavery and child labor. Miria hoped to liberate all sentient life from the oppression designed by those in power to stay in power indefinitely. 

Historians say Miria used the resulting power vacuum to further establish her influence in all of the kingdoms, sending a deluge of loyal devotees to help fix the resulting economic and agricultural collapse. These devotees built more humane practices in the old ones’ stead, while naturally also building in heavy taxation for the divine empire. Her loyalists represented her interests in the kingdoms’ armies, navies, and royal guards.

After the two hundred fifty year long Great Crusade across every corner of Astrana, Miria was satisfied with the state of the kingdoms, and chose to dissolve her divine imperial army. 
## The Age of the Empire
Miria's unification of the people of the world only stuck in the hearts and minds of the many for so long, though; eventually, new wars began, alliances and hostilities took shape, and conflict broke out anew throughout the kingdoms of the world. Only a hundred and seventy years after the dissolution of the first, Miria formed a second Divine Imperial Army, and sent them out across Astrana as peacekeepers. So began the [[Age of the Empire]].

The kingdoms have been living under Miria's rule for thousands of years.
