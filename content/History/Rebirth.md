The transformation of the great desert into a fertile land that could support all kinds of life is widely known in the stories of the world's peoples as the Rebirth. It was so called because it was truly a rebirth of Astrana with a living heart, making passage across the center of its landmass possible, unifying distant peoples and cultures, making new trade routes possible, ushering in an explosion of life on Mir - all thanks to the love and guidance of [Miria], the willspawn of the Mother.
