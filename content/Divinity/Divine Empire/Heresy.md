Heresy in the eyes of the [[Divine Empire|Empire]] consists of the sins one may commit against [[Miria]] and is sure to draw their ire. Necromancy, as it disrupts the natural cycle of life, and the worship of demons are two of the most common forms of heresy.

The Empire has a tenuous relationship with the [[Fey|fey]] due to their unique relationship with divinity, but the separation between civilization and the [[Feywilds|feywilds]] mostly keeps violent conflict to a minimum.
