*(mere-e-uh) | she/her*

Miria is the mother [[Willspawn|goddess]], the God-Empress of [[Mir]], and is widely known as the Mother of Mir. She is a ubiquitous figure in the world's cultures, a reminder of the miracle of life and the beauty of having brought into this world, protected, and cared for. 

The many realms of the land have their own rulers, kingdoms, communes, and townships - a wide variety of governments, but all these realms of [[Astrana]] live under the banner of the Mother of Mir. To call the rule of the world her [[Divine Empire|Empire]] is almost misleading; it implies the existence of opposition, of other rule, but Miria rules alone. Her rule is more than law - it is cultural and spiritual.  She rules the hearts and minds of all the children of Mir.

She has ruled for just over a thousand years, since the [[Rebirth]], when she brought life to the [[Great Desert]] at the heart of [[Astrana]].

Depictions of Miria commonly resemble the ancestry of those who choose to depict her, as it's said that in the flesh, she appears as an ideal mother to the individual.
