The Willspawn are known by many names, but in [[Common]] they are mainly known as gods, spirits, and demons. Despite their [[Divinity (The Will)|divine]] nature, they are neither all-seeing nor all–knowing; they are simply born of [[Divinity (The Will)|the will]] with abilities suiting their conceptual root. 

The value judgments (good, neutral/mixed, and evil) of concepts and ideas within the will are what dictate their Willspawn’s nature. The Willspawn of ideas deemed 'good' are seen as godly, and are commonly known as gods. The Willspawn of ideas with a more complicated sentiment - the grays of life - are mostly known as spirits. Those deemed 'bad', demons. These terms are more a reflection of the concepts and feelings people broadly have about these Willspawn and their cultural reputation than an absolute descriptor of their form or morality.

If there is a shift in the majority opinion, feeling, or knowledge about a concept, this can change both the potency of the corresponding willspawn's magical abilities and how they appear in the world. It is thought that if a new cultural concept is to emerge and plant itself into the will, a Willspawn will be born, especially as it known that there are many Willspawn who are older than others. Some say new concepts all come from the roots of previous ones, meaning Willspawn must be born of others. However, the stories of their origins are varied and don't add up to a clear reality.

While Willspawn can be incredibly influential and exert their will on the world at large, they are still living beings occupying the world of [[Mir]]. Some opt to generally hide from the [[People of Mir|people of Mir]] or haven't been seen in a long time, but there is no evidence of the death of a Willspawn in recorded [[History|history]].
