*Woman | she/her*

Cassandra Erlich is the young adult daughter of [[Ilana Erlich]], and is said to be "cursed with the blessing of prophetic foresight", as if she is an unwilling participant in [[Divination]] magic.
### Appearance
Cassandra is rarely seen outside the Erlich's palatial estate, but she looks like a disheveled version of her mother. Her black hair has grown out for many years, and her posture is poor. She has the elven ears, silver eyes, and slender pointed face of her mother, but much of it is hidden in the curtains of her poorly-kempt hair. 
### Family
Cassandra is the daughter of [[Ilana Erlich]] and some other unknown individual. 
### Prophetic Foresight
Since Cassandra was a young girl, she has been plagued by brief visions of things that always come to pass, with frustratingly little context or extra information beyond clear images and associations.
