*Man | he/him*

Elwin Aluianti is a member of [[Ilana Erlich]]'s Royal Guard, and knows their family well. He was tasked with escorting the fateful party to [[Otari]] for a job in the fishery belonging to [[Tamily Tanderveil]]. 
### Appearance
Elwin is a towering, extremely fit elf with short platinum blonde hair, ivory skin, and striking green eyes. Best estimates would put him in young adulthood, somewhere around one hundred years in his elven lifespan.
### Perspective
Elwin once told the party at risk of being charged with treason that he would sooner see a baby dragon returned home than entrusted to the Erlichs, as he felt that Ilana would find ways to leverage having a baby dragon to either cause great suffering or to simply wield it herself as a tool of oppression. He expressed belief in the importance of duty, but claimed his duty is to protect the Erlichs, not to enable wanton destruction or the suffering of the innocent masses.
