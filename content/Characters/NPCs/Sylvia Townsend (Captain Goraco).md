Sylvia Townsend was a [[Hobbit (Halfling)|hobbit]] citizen of [[Otari]], who was known to the [[Naya]] as Captain Goraco, but her true name and identity remain unknown. She ran operations within the region surrounding [[Otari]], often handling shipments traveling to and from [[Ivrand]] along the north-south route through the [[Northeastern Kingdoms]], as well as occasional east-west shipments heading to [[Brummage]].

She met [[Lizzy and Fizzy]] on the night of the fateful party's post-fishery celebration, and spoke to [[Mor]] and [[Lorelei]] the following morning.

She was killed by the fateful party in the [[Naya]] hideout under the Temple of [[Milue]] in [[Tobo Forest]].
### Appearance
Sylvia was a beautiful red-haired hobbit with amber eyes and a kind but serious face.
