A holiday on the 1st, 337th, or 0th day of the [[Common (Imperial) Calendar|Common Calendar]], depending on who you ask, which celebrates [[Miria]] and the ancient miracle through which she brought life to the heart of [[Astrana]].

Miria's great [[Rebirth]] began on the day before the spring equinox, so the day between Hiemnox and Verora is known as the [[Rebirth Day]].
