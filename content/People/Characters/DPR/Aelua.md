Aelua is a powerful druid of [[Plaitos Forest]], and claims to be both an ex-[[Huntresses|Huntress]] and a daughter of the Moon Spirit, [[Milue]].
### Appearance
Aelua is a fit woodland [[Elf|elf]]. She has long brown hair, big amber eyes, a small pointy nose, and sharp facial features.
