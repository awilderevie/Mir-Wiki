Cassandra Erlich is the young adult daughter of [[Ilana Erlich]], and is said to be "cursed with the blessing of prophetic foresight", as if an unwilling participant in powerful [[Divination]] magic.
### Appearance
Cassandra is rarely seen outside the Erlich's palatial estate, but she appears as a younger, disheveled version of Ilana. Her black hair has grown out for many years, and her posture is poor. She has the elven ears, silver eyes, and slender pointed face of Ilana, but much of it is hidden in the curtains of her poorly-kempt hair. She is known to have [[Elf|elf]] ancestry, but any other lineage is not public knowledge.
### Family
Cassandra is the child of [[Ilana Erlich]], and is said to be born of [[Mirian Conception]].
### Prophetic Foresight
Since Cassandra was a young girl, she has been plagued by brief visions of things that always come to pass, with little context beyond distinct images and loose associations between them.
