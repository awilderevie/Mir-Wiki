Lizzy and Fizzy are two [[Kobold|kobolds]] who were caught by the party under [[Otari]] as part of the group of kobolds living under its fishery, plotting to destroy the town after raising the baby [[Dragons|dragon]] their leader discovered.

They are currently working in the Otari Fishery for [[Tamily Tanderveil]] in order to turn a new leaf and integrate into a better, more loving community.
### Appearance
They are both young kobolds of middling height with blue scales. Lizzy has yellow eyes, and Fizzy green.
