Feywilds are natural areas that have been transformed over many years by the presence of the fey.

The feywilds of [[Mir]] are not a domain with a land separate from [[Astrana]], but a feature dotted through its lands as a consequence of the existence of the [[Fey|fey]]. Their very existence in community changes the land around them through their magic nature, and creates a domain which allows for the perpetuation of their otherwise mortal spirits through multiple bodies and "lifetimes."
