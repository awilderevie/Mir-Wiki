Otari is a small lakeside fishing town in the [[Northeastern Kingdoms]], about a day's ride southeast from [[Ilana Erlich]]'s palatial estate, and just south of [[Tobo Forest]]. It borders [[Lake Roto]].

Otari is populated mainly by [[Hobbit (Halfling)|hobbits]], but there is also a small [[Shoony|shoony]] population, and an even smaller number of [[Kobold|kobolds]] - namely, [[Lizzy and Fizzy]].
### Adventures
In this town where the fateful party's first adventure together took place, word of something sinister affecting Otari's fish production reached the Sovereign Ilana's intelligence network, and the party was consequently sent here to begin working as a team and prove their mettle. First staying in the inn, the party met [[Tamily Tanderveil]], whose fishery had been raided nightly for over a week, stealing much of her daily catch and her preserved fish reserves.

After discovering a secret kobold clan's plot to raise a baby [[Dragons|dragon]] until it was capable of attacking the village, the party thwarted their plans, killing the clan leader and sparing two kobolds named [[Lizzy and Fizzy]], while also saving the young dragon and dubbing it [[Lil' Snappa]]. 

Tamily Tanderveil threw the party a massive party upon their triumphant return, where [[Lorelei Nightwind]] sang an epic tale of the party's heroics that will stick around for years to come.
