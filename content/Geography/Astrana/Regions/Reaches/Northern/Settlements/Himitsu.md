*(him-it-su)*

Himitsu is a small [[Tanuki|tanuki]] village in the [[Yama Mountains]], on the eastern edge of the [[Northern Reach]].

The tanuki of Himitsu have lived there for longer than any of them know for certain, and it is unknown whether they built or simply found the shrine to a long-forgotten visage of the moon spirit, [[Milue]], at its center.

While many tanuki colonies are built on the fringes of major cities, this village is unique in its homogeny. Its existence is funded by tanuki capitalizing on their innate magical talents, doing work of a questionable moral nature in order to afford the niceties and spirits that a village of festival-loving tanuki need.
