The Fellreach is a part of [[Astrana]] which extends out into the southern hemisphere of [[Mir]] from the [[Southern Reach|Southern]] and [[Eastern Reach]]. It is the region of the world with the least [[Divine Empire|Empire]] influence, owing to its distance from the [[Heartlands]], though its northwestern edge lies just across the [[Kalakalan Sea]]. 

Little is truly known about life in the [[Fellreach]], as it is said to be the domain of the monstrous and beastly. Many believe it to be home to the [[Dragons|dragons]] of Mir, but there are many stories of that which lives in the Fell. A journey into the Fell to slay its beasts and prove one's mettle is a common heroic endeavor for those seeking to make a name for themselves.
