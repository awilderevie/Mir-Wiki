The Reaches are the [[Common]] name for the parts of [[Astrana]] outside the [[Heartlands]]. There are five Reaches:
1) [[Northern Reach]]
2) [[Southern Reach]]
3) [[Eastern Reach]]
4) [[Western Reach]]
5) [[Fellreach]]

Life and civilization in the Reaches long pre-date the [[Divine Empire|Empire]], as life in the [[Heartlands]] was only made possible by the [[Rebirth]].
