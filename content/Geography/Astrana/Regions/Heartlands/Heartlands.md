The [[Heartlands]] are, perhaps unsurprisingly, the very heart of [[Astrana]], and consist of the land of the [[Great Desert]] which was [[Rebirth|reborn]] a thousand years ago. The inner Heartlands are the lands of the [[Divine Empire|Empire]], and are home to [[Meldirias]], known as the capital of Mir itself. The realms of the outer Heartlands have a strong relationship with the Empire but are mostly independent, including kingdoms like [[Carunia]].
