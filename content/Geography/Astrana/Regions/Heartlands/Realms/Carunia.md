*(cah-rune-ya)*

Carunia is a monarchy in the northwestern outer [[Heartlands]] known best for its enchanted metalworking. It has a very active trade relationship with [[Meldirias]] and the [[Divine Empire|Empire]], but is famed the world over.

The Queen of Carunia is Croia Granndach, who carries forth the legacy of the Granndach family. Carunia was once a kingdom of little renown before Croia's great grandfather, Finn, married a legendary runic enchanter from [[Priluum]], Lady Inola. Lady Inola founded an enchanting academy in the capital, [[Ducreis]].
