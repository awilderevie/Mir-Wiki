Astrana is the single major landmass of [[Mir]], surrounded wholly by ocean. The coastal and outer regions of Astrana are rich with a remarkable variety of life and environments, as well as home to many pockets of civilization. The massive landmass’ interior was once an incomprehensibly vast [[Great Desert]] that kept the [[Prehistoric Kingdoms|old kingdoms]] of Astrana apart, but it has not been that way in a thousand years, since the [[Rebirth]]. 

There are still pockets of desert that remain from prehistory, and these deserts are fairly large in their own right, but nothing compared to the Great Desert of the distant past.

The wide open ocean's lack of distributed landmass gives rise to incomprehensibly massive and dangerous storms. These storms, best described as mega-monsoons, batter the coasts of Astrana and make it difficult for port cities to flourish on its east and west coasts, particularly around the equator.
