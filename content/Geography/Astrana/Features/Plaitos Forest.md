Plaitos is a massive forest that stretches across the entire northern edge of Astrana, all the way from the northern end of the [[Eastern Reach]] through the [[Northern Reach|Northern]] to the [[Western Reach]]. It spans across multiple mountain ranges and covers an incomprehensibly vast swath of land. Many believe that Plaitos is the oldest forest in the world. There are a handful of ancient great [[Feywilds|feywilds]] contained within its expanse. 

[[Ond]] is the spirit of Plaitos Forest, and is one of the world's great place-spirits.
