*(kuh-lock-uh-lan)*

The Kalakalan Sea is one of the calmest parts of [[Mir]]'s [[ocean]], situated between the [[Southern Reach]] and the western side of the [[Fellreach]]. It is by far the most sailed portion of the [[ocean]] alongside the [[Gulf of Olasse]].
