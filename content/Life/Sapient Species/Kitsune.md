Kitsune are an innately magical, notoriously charismatic people born with the ability to shapechange. They can shapechange between two forms: a fox-like humanoid, and one that depends on their origins. Kitsune raised in isolation or in their true form are generally only capable of shapechanging into an actual fox!
## Physical Description
Kitsune are humanoid fox-like people with alert vulpine ears, pointed snouts, and short, semi-retractable claws on their fingers and toes. Their fur is dense, and is usually red, tan, black, or white in color with counter-shading similar to actual foxes. Kitsune have bushy tails that grow in number as they hone their innate magical abilities, up to as many as nine.

Their shapechanged form is analogous to their true one, with the same age and body type. Their hair and eye color is shared between forms, too.
## Society & Culture
There are a few kitsune-only colonies in the world, but most live immersed in cultures among people of other ancestries. 

Consequently, kitsune are often incredibly charismatic, owing to their lifelong experience of directing people's attentions and feelings a particular way. They often feel like outsiders to these cultures given their hidden double nature. Many are prone to mischief, especially in their younger years.

Kitsune who live immersed in other cultures often have two names: a true name with kitsune roots, and a name they use to blend in with the culture they live in.
## Beliefs
Kitsunes' dual natures often have an impact on their beliefs about the world, with the constant burden of hiding their true self. They are generally quite aware of the reality that many people in the world have a hidden agenda, or at least have something to hide. This can lead some kitsune to become spectacularly cynical.

Many kitsune privately worship [[Yala]], the demon of deception and lying. Others worship [[Sapphos]], the spirit of storytelling. This worship is often kept private as to avoid giving others a clue about one's true nature.
## Other Information
A kitsune's number of tails in their true form reflects their arcane ability. The more tails a kitsune has, the more they have developed their innate magical potential.
