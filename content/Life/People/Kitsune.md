Kitsune are an innately magical, notoriously charismatic people born with the ability to shapechange. They can shapechange between two forms: a fox-headed and tailed humanoid and one that depends on their origins. Kitsune raised around other people are usually capable of transforming into a form like that humanoid without any fox features, but those raised in isolation's other form is often simply a fox. 

There are said to be kitsune-only colonies, but most live among people of other ancestries, often seeing the communities they are a part of as an outsider. 
### Physical Description
Kitsune have alert vulpine ears, pointed snouts, and short, semi-retractable claws on their fingers and toes. Their fur is dense, countershaded, and most often red, tan, black, or white in color. Kitsune have bushy tails that grow in number as they hone their innate magical abilities, to as many as nine.
