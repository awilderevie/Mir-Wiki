The dragons of [[Mir]] are primarily creatures of legend, storied in myth and heroics, but many people of Mir claim to have seen them flying overhead. While they're widely believed to exist, little is known about their true nature in this world.
### Legends
Dragons are said to be creatures of great magic with profound destructive potential. Older legends depict dragons as treasure-hoarding monstrous creatures, but it's thought this may all originate from a single dragon who lived several hundred years ago. 

Some stories concern a secretive civilization, hidden somewhere in the wilds of the world, telling of a language unlike any other on Mir, and even speak of gnomes who are permitted to live with them in a symbiotic partnership, though never permitted to leave. There are also tales of a cult of [[Fleshwarp|fleshwarps]] that are chosen as dragon riders who cut out their tongues so they may only communicate through a kind of bonded telepathy.

Some say this secretive draconic civilization looms high in the sky out over Mir's massive [[Ocean]], but other accounts claim it to be deep in the massive mountain ranges of the world, or even in the [[Fellreach]].
