The dragons of [[Mir]] are creatures of legend, spoken of in stories of grand adventure and heroics, and many people of Mir claim to have seen them flying overhead. While they're widely believed to exist, little is known about their true nature in this world.
### Legends
Dragons are said to be creatures of great magic with profound destructive potential. Older legends depict dragons as treasure-hoarding monstrous creatures, but this is thought to originate from one actual dragon who lived several hundred years ago. 

Recent stories concern their secretive civilization, hidden somewhere in the wilds of the world, telling of a language unlike any other on Mir, and even speaking of gnomes who are permitted to live with them in a symbiotic partnership but are never permitted to leave [[people of Mir]]. There are also tales of a cult of [[Fleshwarp|fleshwarps]] that are chosen as dragon riders, who cut out their tongues so they may only communicate with dragons through a kind of bonding magic.

Some say this secretive civilization looms high in the sky out over Mir's massive ocean, but other accounts claim it to be deep in the massive mountain ranges of the world.
